## Exercise 1 — a function on a finite set

For

$$
X = \{\, 1,\ \bullet,\ \wp,\ \pi,\ * \,\} \qquad \text{and} \qquad Y = \{\, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, \mathord{!} \,\}
$$

we define the function:

$$
f(1) = 10, \quad f(\bullet) = 9, \quad f(\wp) = \mathord{!}, \quad f(\pi) = 10, \quad f(*) = 8.
$$

- Find $f(A)$ and $f^{-1}(B)$, where $A = \{\, \bullet,\ \wp,\ \pi \,\}$ and $B = \{\, 10, \mathord{!} \,\}$.
- Is $f$ **injective** (one-to-one), and is it a function **"onto"** (a **surjection**) from $X$ to $Y$?
- Does there exist a **bijection** from $X$ to $Y$?
- Find $\mathrm{Dom}(f)$ and $\mathrm{Rg}(f)$.

## Exercise 2 — real functions

For $f_i : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, where

$$
f_1(x) = \frac{1}{x-1} \qquad \text{and} \qquad f_2(x) = \sqrt[4]{x} \qquad (i = 1, 2),
$$

determine:

- **(i)** $\mathrm{Dom}(f_i)$
- **(ii)** $\mathrm{Rg}(f_i)$
- **(iii)** examine whether $f_i$ $(i = 1,2)$ is injective (one-to-one) and "onto". Find $f_i^{-1}$ where possible.

For both functions find:

$$
f_2([0, 2]), \qquad f_1((1, +\infty)), \qquad f_2^{-1}([0, 16]), \qquad f_1^{-1}((0, 1)).
$$

## Exercise 3 — counting

**a)** In how many ways can 3 tickets be sold for a compartment in a train? (2nd class — 8 seats)

**b)** In how many ways can one form 8-digit numbers from the digits $\{4, 1, 2, 5\}$?

**c)** In how many ways can 20 people be seated in the first row of a theatre with 20 seats?

**d)** In how many ways can one arrange 3 yellow, 4 white and 3 black balls in a row?

**e)** From 4 women and 6 men, in how many ways can one create a 5-person group with 2 women and 3 men?
