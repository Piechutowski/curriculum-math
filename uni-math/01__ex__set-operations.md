# Discrete Mathematics — Exercises 1: Set Operations

> Translated from the handwritten exercise sheet `pdf/Ćwiczenia1.pdf`.

> **Note on symbols:** in Exercise 1 the teacher uses small doodles as set elements — a little flower and a four-leaf clover. They are rendered here as $\circledast$ (the flower) and $\clubsuit$ (the clover).

## Exercise 1

For the sets

$$
A = \{\ast,\ \square,\ 1,\ \blacksquare,\ \circledast\}, \qquad
B = \{\heartsuit,\ \square,\ \pi\}, \qquad
C = \{\ast,\ \circledast\}
$$

with the universe

$$
U = \{\ast,\ \square,\ 1,\ \blacksquare,\ \circledast,\ \pi,\ \heartsuit,\ \clubsuit\},
$$

find

$$
A \cup B, \quad A \cap B, \quad A \setminus B, \quad B \setminus A, \quad A', \quad B', \quad A \oplus B.
$$

Is $C \subseteq A$, and is $C \subseteq B$?

Find $C \times B$ and $B \times C$.

Find $\mathcal{P}(B)$ and $\mathcal{P}(\emptyset)$.

## Exercise 2

For

$$
A = \{-1\} \cup \left(-\tfrac{1}{2},\ \tfrac{2}{3}\right] \qquad \text{and} \qquad B = [0, 1)
$$

find

$$
A \cup B, \quad A \cap B, \quad A \setminus B, \quad B \setminus A, \quad A', \quad B'
$$

(here $U = \mathbb{R} = (-\infty, +\infty)$), and also $A \times B$, $B \times A$.

## Exercise 3

For

$$
A = \{(x, y) : x^2 + y^2 < 1\}
$$

$$
B = \{(x, y) : x \geq 0\}
$$

and the universe

$$
U = \mathbb{R}^2 = \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R} = \{(x, y) : x \in \mathbb{R} \ \text{and} \ y \in \mathbb{R}\}
$$

(the plane), find

$$
A \cup B, \quad A \cap B, \quad A', \quad B', \quad A \setminus B, \quad B \setminus A
$$

and

$$
A \oplus B = (A \cup B) \setminus (A \cap B).
$$

For

$$
A \times B = \{\, ((x, y), (\bar{x}, \bar{y})) : (x, y) \in A \ \text{and} \ (\bar{x}, \bar{y}) \in B \,\}
$$

— why can $A \times B$ not be visualized?
